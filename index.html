About

Roseburg-based. Self-taught. Built to ship.

I'm based in Roseburg, Oregon; a small town where the web work gets done face-to-face, not through cold outreach at scale. I'm a self-directed, full-stack developer working across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node, Python, and REST APIs, and I learn by shipping real things rather than working through tutorials in isolation.

Every site I build gets run through PageSpeed before it's ever pitched. I don't say a site is faster. I show the number, before and after. That standard doesn't stop at client work: Pantry Roulette and Queued have both gone through the parts of a real product that never show up in a screenshot. DNS, accessibility audits, semantic versioning, payment integration, actual users. If I build it, it ships complete, not half-finished.

I don't reuse a client's design, either. Every business gets a visual identity built for its own category and character, never a reskin of the last job. And when a project's done, you get real ownership of it: real hosting access, real admin credentials, no dependency on me to make basic edits.

Services

What I do and what I don't.

Most clients work with me month to month: I keep the site running and build up their local search presence. Custom builds are there when a new site is the right call. Every engagement starts with an audit.

In scope

  • ＋

    An SEO audit comes first on every engagement: your site, Google Business Profile, and listings, measured before any work starts. That way the work goes where it's actually needed.

  • ＋

    Monthly site management: maintenance, updates, content changes, and small fixes, handled every month so the site doesn't slowly break after launch.

  • ＋

    Local SEO retainers: ongoing Google Business Profile management (categories, service areas, photos, posts) plus citation building and cleanup, so your business shows up the same everywhere people search.

  • ＋

    Tracking and monthly reporting in plain language: what changed, what moved, and what's next.

  • ＋

    Custom builds when you need one: mobile-first sites built from scratch for restaurants, retail, and local trades, measured with PageSpeed before delivery. No page-builder templates.

  • ＋

    Client-owned hosting and admin access, every time. No locked-in dependency on me.

Out of scope — by design

  • −

    Paid ad management (Google Ads, Facebook Ads).

    That's a specialized discipline with real budget risk attached. What I guarantee is the site: fast and reliable. Search rankings aren't something anyone can honestly guarantee, so I measure your local SEO every month and report what moved. I partner with dedicated ad specialists for the rest.
